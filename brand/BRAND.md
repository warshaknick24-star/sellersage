# SellerSage brand guide

Source: MKTG 7011 weekly worksheets (Weeks 1–5). Use this guide when you write copy or design anything for the site, ads or outreach.

## Name and tagline

- **Name:** SellerSage. Write it as one word in camel case. The wordmark colors "Seller" sage green and "Sage" gold.
- **Tagline:** *Data-Driven Growth for Your Shop.*
- **Ad headline / hero:** *More Sales. Less Guesswork.*
- **Primary call to action:** *See your free shop audit.*
- **Why the name works:** "Seller" names the audience. "Sage" means a trusted advisor, and the sage plant brings in green and growth. The name is short, easy to say, and free of jargon.

## Positioning statement

> To small and medium-sized Etsy sellers who want to grow their shop but feel unsure how to use AI, automation, or their own sales data, SellerSage is the personalized marketing consulting and automation service that provides hands-on branding, marketing automation, and data-driven selling strategy, because we have AI and data science experience from Siemens and Paychex and a personal commitment to using AI to amplify your authentic voice, but never to replace it.

## Brand adjectives

Trustworthy · knowledgeable · human · personal · efficient · empowering

## Voice

- First person ("I"). Nick is the brand.
- Plain language for older, less tech-savvy sellers. No jargon, no hype.
- Anti-"AI slop": AI works behind the scenes, and the seller's voice and real photos come first.
- Make honest claims only: no guaranteed sales, invented testimonials, or .99 pricing.

Typography is a clean, heavy sans-serif (system UI / Segoe UI), matching the logo wordmark.

## Offer ladder (Week 5)

| Package | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Free shop audit | $0 | Free first fix (reciprocity), entry to the funnel |
| Shop Refresh | $300 | Entry package |
| Refresh + 1 month | $550 | Decoy |
| Refresh + 3 months | $600 | Recommended / best value |
| Full Brand Build | $1,500 | Price anchor: custom site, automated outreach, full rehaul |

Unit economics live in `lib/packages.mjs`: $40/hr, $15/mo tooling, and 1 in 4 audits converting, for an $80 acquisition cost.

## Funnel

1. **Awareness:** Facebook ad + direct outreach, with consistent branding (mere exposure).
2. **Consideration:** free shop audit and the instant listing check.
3. **Conversion:** one clear recommendation (avoids choice overload), no long-term contract.
4. **Loyalty:** 30/60/90-day numbers check-ins that tie back to the audit.
