A quick word about AI

Etsy is filling up with AI-generated junk: fake product photos, listings that all sound the same. Buyers have noticed, and they don’t like it. I don’t either.

I do use AI and data tools, the same way an accountant uses a spreadsheet. They help me research what buyers search for and spot what’s holding a listing back.
